# Worked example: a fictional cross-market organization

**SYNTHETIC TEACHING EXAMPLE.** Example Meridian Organization and Avery Example are fictional. No search or AI-engine measurements were collected for this exercise. The records below demonstrate decisions and handoffs, not client outcomes.

## Scope

Entity: Example Meridian Organization. Audience: business leaders evaluating research services. Markets/languages chosen for the exercise: United Kingdom/English and France/French. Decision: whether the public identity and its claims are sufficiently clear to begin a discovery audit.

## 1. Baseline plan

Create two prompt sets covering identity, services and source verification. The [baseline CSV](../templates/baseline-log.csv) is intentionally header-only. Collection status: NOT STARTED. Mention rate, citation rate and accuracy metrics: NOT MEASURED.

## 2. Narrative decision

Proposed description: "Example Meridian Organization publishes research notes on organizational knowledge." This is a fictional reference description, not a statement about a real company. A proposed "market leader" claim is withheld because no supporting evidence has been supplied.

## 3. Entity foundation

The [identity record](../templates/identity-record.json) establishes the fictional name and reserved example.org URL. The French-language description would require a human-approved translation. A hypothetical outdated job title is logged for investigation rather than silently overwritten.

## 4. Evidence map

The [claim-register template](../templates/claim-register.csv) has no asserted real claims. For a training run, a reviewer could add a clearly synthetic owned publication and mark its relationship as owned. That would not count as independent validation. Companion tool examples contain separately labeled synthetic records.

## 5. Implementation plan

Proposed tasks: confirm canonical biography, check page directives, create a source-linked description and review cross-language consistency. None is reported as completed or as affecting rankings. A technical HTTP response alone would not confirm indexation.

## 6. Governance

Assign an identity owner and evidence reviewer; schedule a repeatable baseline once sources and scope are approved. A role change opens a new review item. Follow-up result: NOT MEASURED.

## What this example demonstrates

A useful engagement can identify publication decisions and evidence gaps before it has quantitative results. The final deliverable must preserve that distinction.
