# Limits and honest interpretation

- The method structures work. It does not control search rankings, model training, retrieval, wording, citations or recommendations.
- A public repository or a structured-data file does not prove that a search engine indexed a page or that an AI provider used it.
- Consistency with a canonical record is not truth verification; the record itself can be wrong or outdated.
- A citation is not necessarily corroboration. Independence, relevance and evidential strength need separate human review.
- The toolkit supplies no measured market benchmark, client case study or externally validated authority score.
- Fictional examples are teaching material. They are not anonymized client results and must not be quoted as observed outcomes.
- Automated tools process supplied information; unavailable sources and undisclosed model details remain unknown.
- Monitoring can detect sampled changes. Causal claims about improvements require a stronger research design.
- Website legal, financial, privacy and regulated professional issues may require relevant specialists.

The operating approach should be adapted to the entity, scope and evidence available. Document adaptations so others can understand the limits of a result.
